The Big Challenge

"Jawad, my boy. The people of our city have lost their most precious golden things. Sneak into 30 houses, find them, and return every treasure. Do this without being caught, and you will come home a true hero!"

Super Jawad is a brave little hero. His father gave him a big challenge to prove he is responsible and independent.

Remember: you are not a thief - you're a hero! Nobody must see you, because the surprise has to be perfect. 🤫

How to Play

🕹️WASD or Joystick to sneak around
💨Shift / RUN button - fast but noisy!
👀Stay out of the yellow vision cones of residents
🚪Stand still in wardrobes, curtains, plants and boxes to hide
🌑Dark rooms hide you. Flip light switches with Space
📷Cameras trigger the alarm - switch them off at the CAM box
🐶Sleeping dogs wake if you run nearby - tiptoe!
🗝️Colored keys open matching locked doors
💎Find the special treasure to open the EXIT
⭐Collect all the gold for 3 stars

If someone catches you, don't worry - they'll just walk you outside and you can try again!
